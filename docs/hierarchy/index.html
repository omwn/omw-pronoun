OMW pronoun hierarchy

Each concept is shown as its features: 1eDu is “we two, not you”. A concept sits below every concept whose features contain its own, within its part of speech, so some concepts appear under two hypernyms. See FEATURES.md for the algebra.

Words marked ◇ come from existing wordnets and have not been checked as pronouns: words of the synsets for the concepts linked to the Interlingual Index (here, there, now, then, both, so), and of the Princeton synsets the NTU wordnet tags as pronouns (all, each, everywhere). A wordnet synset can hold words that are not pro-forms, such as Indonesian waktu “time” for then.
